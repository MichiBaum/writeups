## Idea

### Pros and cons
Pros
- It is less theoretical than the [Spanish](./ruy-lópez.md) or [Italian](./italian_opening.md) openings
- It avoids [Petrov's Defense](./petrovs_defense.md)
- White has strategically clear formations

Cons
- It puts less immediate pressure on Black
- It blocks the c-pawn, making c2-c3 to prepare the d2-d4 push more difficult
