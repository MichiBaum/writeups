# Directive

What Are Directives?
Directives are classes that add behavior to elements in your Angular applications. In simpler terms, they are like functions that can modify how elements are rendered or behave in the DOM.

Key Features:
- They manipulate the DOM.
- They help extend HTML’s default functionality.
- Directives are reusable across components.
